DuoFrame Pro — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

The short version

DuoFrame Pro does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data. Everything stays on your phone.

What the app uses

  • Camera — for the live viewfinder and to take photos. Images are processed on your device.
  • Photos (add only) — to save the pictures you take to your photo library. The app does not read your existing photos.
  • Settings — your choices (format, timer and so on) are stored on your device so they are remembered.

Purchases

The optional RAW unlock is an in-app purchase handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details. Apple tells the app only whether the purchase is active on your Apple ID.

Analytics, ads and tracking

None. The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code and makes no network requests of its own.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this ever changes, the new policy will be posted here and the App Store privacy label updated before the change ships.
